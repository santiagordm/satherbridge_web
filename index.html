Results

Selected work.

FTSE-listed trading platform +345%paid-media conversion rate in one quarter

Turning around a multi-market marketing organization

Context

Marketing had the people and the budget to grow faster. The missing pieces were shared goals tied to business impact and clear ownership of regional budgets.

What I did
  • Org design: restructured the marketing leadership and the team in the first two weeks.
  • Providers: cancelled underperforming agency and supplier contracts.
  • Ways of working: two OKRs replacing task-based goals, plus impact KPIs.
  • Measurement: a measurement framework built from scratch.
  • Budget: the first regional budget built on unit economics.
Results

Paid-media conversion rate +345% in one quarter. Vendor costs down 15%+ year on year. A simpler structure, clear accountability and a handover package the team now owns. Team survey at handover: 4.4/5 (8 responses).

Adevinta 12+marketplace brands on one positioning playbook

Marketing excellence across 11 markets

Context

One of the world's largest online classifieds groups. Local marketing teams worked at different maturity levels, and the central team had no line authority over them. After the eBay Classifieds acquisition, two marketing teams had to merge.

What I did
  • Built a center of excellence: capability assessments, playbooks (positioning, SEO, CRM), training and a community of 50+ marketers.
  • Launched the new Adevinta corporate brand in under 3 months, ahead of the IPO.
  • Merged the two teams and brought performance marketing in-house.
Results

Internal satisfaction above 4.5/5. SEO coverage from under half of the marketplaces to 100% in 12 months. +10% conversion from the CRM playbook. €5M+ in synergies.

Spanish energy-tech scale-up ABMbuilt from scratch for a B2B scale-up

Building ABM and go-to-market from scratch

Context

A growth-stage B2B company with no ABM capability. Sales and marketing measured activity, not outcomes.

What I did

Audited marketing and growth practices. Redesigned segmentation, targeting and the value proposition per segment. Built ABM and sales alignment, plus a framework to prioritize GTM initiatives.

Results

A clear map of segments with a value proposition for each, an ABM motion that sales and marketing run together, and a shared way to decide which GTM initiatives go first.

About

Santiago Rodríguez de Miguel

As a kid, I loved connect-the-dots drawings. Much like in work and life, a more interesting picture emerges when you bring disciplines, people and cultures together.

I’ve spent more than 20 years leading marketing and growth in global corporations and fast-growing businesses, helping them adapt to change and find new ways to grow.

At Adevinta, one of the world’s largest online classifieds groups, I spent eight years as Director of Growth and Head of Marketing. I led a team of 15 across 11 markets in Europe and Latin America, integrated eBay Classifieds marketing teams and served as interim CMO in Italy during its shift to a transactional model.

Earlier in my career, I worked in brand and acquisition at L’Oréal (Lancôme), Privalia and Vodafone. At Tansley (Milbby), I rebuilt the eCommerce channel. Since 2024, I’ve worked as an Executive Marketing Advisor and Fractional CMO, helping companies rethink marketing for the AI era. I also co-founded an AI-native investor education platform.

I studied Law and Business Administration at ICADE (Comillas Pontifical University) and completed my MBA at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business.
